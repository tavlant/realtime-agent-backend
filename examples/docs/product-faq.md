# Product FAQ

## What plans exist?
There are three plans: Free (1 workspace), Team (up to 25 seats) and Enterprise
(SSO, audit logs and a 99.9% uptime SLA).

## How do refunds work?
Annual plans can be refunded pro rata within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are
not refunded but can be cancelled at any time.

## Where is data stored?
Customer data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt) by default; US hosting is available on
the Enterprise plan.
